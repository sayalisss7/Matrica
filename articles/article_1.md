# Paper Rex Secures Valorant Champions 2025 Spot

Paper Rex, one of the most dominant teams in the Pacific region, has officially secured their spot in the Valorant Champions 2025. Known for their aggressive playstyle and unconventional agent comps, PRX demonstrated exceptional form during the VCT Pacific Stage 2. With star players like 'something' and 'Jinggg', their entry into the global tournament makes them a major contender for the championship title.

Sponsorship Note: Secretlab and HyperX have recently expanded their partnerships with Paper Rex, anticipating massive viewership numbers from the SEA region.
