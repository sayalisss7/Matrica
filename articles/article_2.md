# Sentinels Announce New Roster Changes for 2025

North American powerhouse Sentinels have announced sweeping changes to their starting roster ahead of the 2025 VCT Americas season. Seeking to rebuild after a mixed performance in 2024, the team has acquired two rising stars from the Tier 2 scene. The management believes these changes will bring fresh tactical depth.
